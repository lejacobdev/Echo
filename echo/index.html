This browser can't run Echo

Echo needs microphone access and the Web Audio API, which this browser doesn't support (or you're on an insecure, non-HTTPS connection).

Try the latest Chrome, Safari, or Firefox on this device, served over HTTPS or from localhost.

Microphone access needed

Echo can't find your partner's phone without listening for its reply chirp. You denied (or dismissed) the microphone permission prompt.

Enable microphone access for this site in your browser's site settings, then reload the page.

How it works

  1. Both people open this page on their phones.
  2. One picks Seeker, the other picks Responder.
  3. Hold both phones touching each other and tap Calibrate.
  4. Now walk apart. Tap Ping to see the live distance between you.

Honest limitations

  • Accuracy is realistically 1–3 meters, not centimeters — this is a proximity aid, not survey-grade positioning.
  • Effective range is roughly 5–10 meters in quiet conditions, less in loud or echoey environments.
  • No directional bearing yet — you get distance only, not "which way." That needs multiple synced mics (native app, stretch goal).
  • Uses a brief, near-inaudible ultrasonic chirp only while this page is open and a role is selected. Nothing runs in the background or without you tapping a button.
